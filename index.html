1. Motivation, Scope, and Objectives

1.1 Workshop Topic and Scope

Closing the loop means making perception serve action and action improve perception. Task goals guide multimodal sensing; perception estimates geometry, contact, dynamics, and uncertainty; manipulation plans and regulates interaction. Each action changes the scene and generates evidence for the next decision. We connect touch, force, audio, olfaction, vision, and proprioception through their contribution to task-relevant inference and adaptive physical interaction.

1.2 Relevance to ICRA and Why Now

Reusable VLA policies [1], dynamic tactile representations [2], and on-demand multisensor systems [3] make joint design timely. The challenge is to connect observations, actionable state estimates, and real-time planning and control under changing contacts and sensor reliability. ICRA unites hardware, perception, learning, and control researchers.

1.3 Main Scientific Questions and Expected Outcomes

How should tasks shape sensing and data acquisition? What must multimodal representations and world models predict for effective planning? How should manipulation exploit uncertainty, gather evidence, and recover from failure? Discussion will link task success, estimation quality, modality contribution, latency, and recovery to evaluation guidance and research priorities.
