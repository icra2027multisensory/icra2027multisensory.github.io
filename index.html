Workshop Overview

Closing the loop means making perception serve action and action improve perception. Task goals guide multimodal sensing; perception estimates geometry, contact, dynamics, and uncertainty; manipulation plans and regulates interaction. Each action changes the scene and generates evidence for the next decision.

We connect touch, force, audio, olfaction, vision, and proprioception through their contribution to task-relevant inference and adaptive physical interaction. ICRA unites hardware, perception, learning, and control researchers.

Questions for the Workshop

  • How should task goals shape sensing and data acquisition?
  • What should multimodal representations and world models predict for effective planning?
  • How can manipulation use uncertainty, gather evidence, and recover from failure?

Discussion will connect task success, estimation quality, modality contribution, latency, and recovery to evaluation guidance and research priorities.
