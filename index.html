About the Workshop

Robots need to decide what to sense as they manipulate an object. Vision, touch, force, sound, and proprioception provide different information about the object and its response to an action. The workshop focuses on how robots can combine that information to choose and adjust their next action.

The proposed half-day workshop connects multisensory robot learning with task-driven closed-loop manipulation. Topics span sensor fusion, representation learning, planning, and adaptive control, with invited talks, contributed presentations, posters, demos, and a panel discussion.
