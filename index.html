Closing the Loop: Task-Driven Multimodal Sensing, Perception, and Manipulation

Closing the loop means making perception serve action and action improve perception. Task goals guide multimodal sensing. Perception estimates geometry, contact, dynamics, and uncertainty, while manipulation plans and regulates interaction. Each action changes the scene and generates evidence for the next decision. We connect touch, force, audio, olfaction, vision, and proprioception through their contribution to task-relevant inference and adaptive physical interaction.

Reusable VLA policies, dynamic tactile representations, and on-demand multisensor systems make joint design timely. The challenge is to connect observations, actionable state estimates, and real-time planning and control under changing contacts and sensor reliability. ICRA unites hardware, perception, learning, and control researchers.
