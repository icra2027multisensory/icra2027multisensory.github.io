01 / THE QUESTION

Beyond seeing.
Learning through interaction.

Cameras and language can describe a task, but they cannot always reveal slip, contact, compliance, hidden states, or physical failure. Robots need to combine sensory evidence and use action to reduce uncertainty.

This proposed workshop brings together two complementary research directions: learning across diverse senses and closing the loop between task, sensing, inference, and control.

“What information does a robot need next—and which action will reveal it?”
