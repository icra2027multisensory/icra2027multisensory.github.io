Tentative Half-Day Program

The proposed workshop is in person from 08:30 to 12:30. The assigned ICRA workshop day is pending.

TimeSession
08:30–08:40Opening
08:40–10:00Four invited talks, 20 minutes each including Q&A
10:00–10:20Up to five contributed spotlights
10:20–11:35Poster and demonstration session with coffee
11:35–12:20Panel and audience debate
12:20–12:30Synthesis

Talks will connect sensing hardware, actionable perception, and manipulation and control. Contributed presentations will examine the interfaces between these areas. Unused time will support discussion.

The panel will discuss how multimodal representations connect to control, the role of touch, audio, and olfaction beyond vision and language, robustness to uncertain or missing observations, and data and evaluation needs for real-world manipulation.

Invited Talk Themes

Four invited talks are planned. Speaker names, affiliations, and final topics are still to be confirmed.

  1. Multimodal sensing
  2. Perception and state estimation
  3. Representations and world models
  4. Manipulation and feedback control

Posters and Demonstrations

The proposed capacity is up to 20 posters, including at most 10 tabletop demonstrations.

Videos and recorded demonstrations are welcome when transporting hardware is impractical.

Organizer Requests

  • Theater seating, projection and audio, a lectern, and an audience microphone.
  • Space for up to 20 posters and up to 10 small demonstration tables with standard power, subject to venue availability.
  • Demonstrators will provide specialist equipment and recorded backups. No special seating reconfiguration is requested.

The event is planned in person. It does not require dedicated wired Ethernet or conference-provided hybrid support. Optional streaming would use organizer-supplied arrangements and require presenter permission.
