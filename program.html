3. Program and Format

3.1 Tentative Half-day Program.

In person, 08:30–12:30. 08:30–08:40 opening; 08:40–10:00 four invited talks (20 minutes each, including Q&A); 10:00–10:20 up to five spotlights; 10:20–11:35 posters/demos and coffee; 11:35–12:20 panel and audience debate; 12:20–12:30 synthesis. Talks connect sensing hardware, actionable perception, and manipulation/control; contributed presentations examine their interfaces. Unused time supports discussion.
