Important Dates and Logistics

Planned Submission Timeline

Planned deadlines are eight weeks before the event for submissions, five for decisions, and three for final papers.

Room Layout and Equipment

We request theater seating, projection/audio, a lectern and an audience microphone, space for up to 20 posters, and up to ten small demo tables with standard power, subject to venue availability. Demonstrators supply specialist equipment and recorded backups. No special seating reconfiguration is required.

Remote or Hybrid Services (If Applicable)

The event is planned in person and does not require dedicated wired Ethernet or conference-provided hybrid support. Optional streaming would use organizer-supplied arrangements and presenter permission.
