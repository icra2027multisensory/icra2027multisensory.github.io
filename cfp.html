IEEE ICRA 2027 · PROPOSED HALF-DAY WORKSHOP

Call for Papers.

Multisensory Robot Manipulation: Sensing, Learning, and Acting in the Loop

Draft only. This workshop has not yet been accepted. The final title, CFP, submission link, dates, paper lengths, review policy, and proceedings policy require organizer approval.

ABOUT THE CALL

What we hope to explore

We invite the robotics community to explore how sensing, perception, learning, and control can work together during physical interaction. Cameras and language provide valuable information, but many manipulation tasks require a robot to infer properties through contact, sound, force, proprioception, and purposeful exploratory actions.

The proposed workshop connects multisensory representation learning with closed-loop, task-driven decision making. Contributions may investigate individual modalities, systems that combine them, or methods for deciding when additional evidence is worth gathering.

Suggested topics

  • Multisensory foundation models and world models for embodied manipulation
  • Vision–touch–force–audio–language–action learning and cross-modal transfer
  • Active perception, information gathering, and task-driven sensing policies
  • Adaptive manipulation, feedback control, and uncertainty-aware planning
  • Learning with missing, unreliable, conflicting, or asynchronous sensors
  • Hardware, multimodal datasets, benchmarks, and reproducible evaluation
  • Failure prediction, recovery, embodiment transfer, and real-world deployment
  • Position papers identifying open problems and future research directions

Proposed contributions

The original proposals welcome a broad range of contributions, including completed research, work in progress, negative results, hardware demonstrations, datasets, benchmarks, position papers, and relevant recently published work. Organizers still need to confirm which categories will be accepted and their paper lengths, review criteria, and archival status.

Participation and engagement

The proposed half-day format combines invited talks, contributed spotlights, a poster and demonstration session, and a moderated panel. We aim to welcome early-career researchers and participants across sensing hardware, computer vision, robot learning, manipulation, and control. Final participation and review details are pending.

How to submit

Not available yet. Please do not submit a workshop paper through the ICRA full workshop-proposal portal. If the workshop is accepted, the organizing team will publish a dedicated submission link, timeline, required format, and proceedings policy on this page.
