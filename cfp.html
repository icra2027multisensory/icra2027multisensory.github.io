Call for Papers

We invite 2–4-page original research/work-in-progress papers and 1–2-page demo, dataset, negative-result, or position papers, excluding references. Two nonconflicted reviewers assess task relevance, evidence, clarity, and discussion value. Accepted papers are intended for archival workshop proceedings, with arrangements to be confirmed. Up to five receive spotlights.

Topics of Interest

  • Multimodal sensing
  • Perception and state estimation
  • Representations and world models
  • Manipulation and feedback control

Submission Guidelines

We plan archival workshop proceedings for accepted original papers. Before the call is issued, the publication route, eligibility of short contributions, copyright/license, and camera-ready timetable must be confirmed. Submissions must satisfy originality and prior/concurrent publication rules. Publisher approval and optional indexing are separate; IEEE Xplore inclusion is not assumed.

Important Dates

Planned deadlines are eight weeks before the event for submissions, five for decisions, and three for final papers.
