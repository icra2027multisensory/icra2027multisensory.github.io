Call for Contributions

We welcome work on task-driven multimodal sensing, actionable perception, and adaptive manipulation. Contributions may address an individual stage of the loop or connect sensing, state estimation, representations, and physical interaction in an integrated system.

Full workshop scope and themes →

Contribution Formats

Research and work in progressOriginal papers of 2–4 pages, excluding references.
Demos, datasets, negative results, and position papersShort papers of 1–2 pages, excluding references.

Review and Selection

Two non-conflicted reviewers will assess each submission for task relevance, evidence, clarity, and value for discussion. Up to five accepted contributions will receive short spotlights before the poster session.

Submissions authored by organizers or advisors will be assigned to an independent coordinator and reviewers outside the organizing and advisory teams.

Publication Plan

The proposal plans archival proceedings for accepted original papers. Before the call is issued, the publication route, eligibility of short contributions, copyright and license terms, and camera-ready schedule must be confirmed. Submissions must follow originality and prior or concurrent publication rules.

IEEE Xplore inclusion is not assumed.
