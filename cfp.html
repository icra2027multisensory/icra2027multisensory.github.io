Draft Call for Papers

Proposed ICRA 2027 Workshop on Multisensory Robot Manipulation

Important: This CFP is a draft. Submission instructions, deadlines, and formats are not final until the workshop is approved and the organizers confirm the details.

Home Scope Topics Submission Types Important Dates

Scope

We invite work on robot perception, representation, and manipulation that uses or studies multiple sensing modalities, especially in closed-loop settings where sensing informs action and action changes what should be sensed next.

Topics

  • Multisensory manipulation and embodied AI
  • Active perception and task-driven sensing
  • Tactile, force, audio, and proprioceptive sensing
  • Multimodal representation learning for robotics
  • Sensor fusion and uncertainty-aware decision making
  • Adaptive control and closed-loop planning
  • Benchmarks, datasets, and robot systems
  • Negative results, lessons learned, and system insights

Planned Submission Types

The exact contribution formats are still being finalized. The organizers are currently considering combinations of:

  • Short papers
  • Full papers
  • Work-in-progress / extended abstracts
  • Demos or system papers
  • Previously published work relevant to the workshop theme

Whether the workshop will use archival or non-archival submissions is still under discussion.
