Call for Papers

We invite work on robot perception, representation, and manipulation that uses or studies multiple sensing modalities, especially in closed-loop settings where sensing informs action and action changes what should be sensed next.

Topics of Interest

  • Multisensory manipulation and embodied AI
  • Active perception and task-driven sensing
  • Tactile, force, audio, and proprioceptive sensing
  • Multimodal representation learning for robotics
  • Sensor fusion and uncertainty-aware decision making
  • Adaptive control and closed-loop planning
  • Benchmarks, datasets, and robot systems
  • Negative results, lessons learned, and system insights
