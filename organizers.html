Organizing Team

The team has 12 organizers and 11 advisory-board members, with experience across sensing, perception, learning, manipulation, and control in Asia, Europe, North America, and Australia.

Their work spans joint sensor, perception, and control design, multisensory representation learning and transfer, and robotic systems. Their combined experience connects sensor observability and data acquisition to the information a learned manipulation policy needs.

Prior Organizing Experience

Wen Jung Li was General Co-Chair of IROS 2019. Ajmal Mian chaired ACCV 2018 and DICTA 2019 and 2024. Xiang Li co-organized the ICRA 2024 Human-Robot Co-Manipulation workshop. Quan Xiong co-organized the RoboSoft 2026 Soft Robotics Meets the Sea workshop. Jianshu Zhou has held session-chair and organizing roles at ICRA, ICCA, and RCAR. Wei Sun and Jian Liu bring conference-committee and invited-session experience.

Jiawei Ma, Baoru Huang, and Anh Nguyen organized ActiVis at CVPR 2026. Tung D. Ta, Anh Nguyen, and Baoru Huang organized the IROS 2025 Foundation Models for Robotic Design workshop. Baoru Huang also co-organized RoDGE on robotic data generation and evaluation. Ngan Le, Anh Nguyen, Kashu Yamazaki, and Khoa Vo contributed to ACCV 2024 LAMM on robust, trustworthy, and cost-optimized multimodal learning. This experience includes peer review, oral and poster programs, and cross-community recruitment.
